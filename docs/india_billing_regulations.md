# Indian Healthcare Billing Regulations Context

This document outlines the statutory framework governing hospital billing in India.

## 1. Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW), Government of India.
- **Role**: Publishes rate schedules for inpatient procedures, ICU care, room rents, and specialist consultations. Widely adopted as the legal benchmark by Indian High Courts and Insurance Regulators (IRDAI) for reasonable charges.
- **Key Directive**: ICU package rates are composite tariffs covering resident medical officer monitoring, nursing care, and basic consumables.

## 2. National Health Authority (NHA) & Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY
- Establishes standardized health benefit packages (HBP 2.2 / 2022) with pre-fixed procedure packages prohibiting itemized unbundling.

## 3. National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) & DPCO
- **Department**: Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers.
- **Orders**:
  - **Cardiac Stents**: Fixed price caps on Bare Metal Stents and Drug Eluting Stents (DES).
  - **Orthopedic Knee Implants**: Price caps under Section 19 of DPCO 2013.
  - **Medical Consumables**: Trade margin rationalization on PPE kits, surgical masks, and oxygen concentrators.

## 4. State Clinical Establishments Acts & Circulars
- State governments (e.g., Delhi, Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal) have notified directives prohibiting hospitals from billing:
  - **Biomedical Waste Disposal Surcharge**: Prohibited from being billed to patients as an itemized fee; must be absorbed in institutional overheads.
  - **Admission File / Registration Fee**: Flagged as unfair trade practice under the Consumer Protection Act.
  - **Infusion Pump & Monitoring Surcharges**: When the patient is already paying ICU charges.
